SAMPLES
-------

Salamander Grand Piano V3 (public/samples/grand)
  Recorded by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5). Licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  Source: SalamanderGrandPianoV3_44.1khz16bit.tar.bz2 from
  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  Changes: velocity layers 4, 7, 10, 13 and 16 (of 16) for every third semitone
  A0–C8, and the 88 release (hammer/damper) noises; folded to mono, shortened
  with a fade-out and encoded as VBR MP3 (src/audio/build-samples.mjs).

Salamander Grand Piano samples (public/samples/piano, fallback)
  Recorded by Alexander Holm. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). MP3 subset as distributed by
  the Tone.js project (https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).

Harpsichord samples (public/samples/harpsichord)
  "Harpsichord, French" from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL),
  Versilian Studios LLC. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); credited with thanks.
  Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL (Chordophones/Zithers/Harpsichord,
  French: Sustains rr1 and Releases). Changes: folded to mono, shortened with a
  fade-out and encoded as VBR MP3 (src/audio/build-samples.mjs).

Room impulse response and metronome sounds are synthesised in code
(src/audio/reverb.js, src/audio/metronome.js); no third-party recordings.

MUTOPIA CATALOGUE (experimental "Browse Mutopia" page)
  public/songs/mutopia-index.json lists titles, composers, licences and links of
  openly licensed Mutopia Project pieces (https://www.mutopiaproject.org,
  github.com/MutopiaProject/MutopiaProject). Pieces opened from that page are
  downloaded from GitHub and converted in the browser; their licence is shown
  with each piece. They are not bundled with the app.

Hand transcriptions in songs/*.notes (compiled to public/songs/*.musicxml)
carry the licence stated in each file's header.

are not committed, and are the responsibility of whoever adds them.
